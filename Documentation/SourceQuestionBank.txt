NUCLEAR ROUND
GROUP CHALLENGES
========================

1. THE ATTRACTION VOTE

Everyone secretly writes down the person in the room they find most attractive.

Reveal only the vote totals, with everyone's agreement.

2. THE SECRET CRUSH

Everyone writes down whether they've ever had a crush on someone present.

Reveal the total number of yes answers, not the identities.

3. THE COUPLE EXPERIMENT

The group nominates two willing people who would make an unexpectedly good couple.

They improvise their first date for two minutes.

4. THE BRUTAL FIRST IMPRESSION

Everyone writes down their first impression of the player in the middle.

The player reads them aloud and tries to guess who wrote each one.

5. THE ANONYMOUS CONFESSION

Everyone writes down something embarrassing they've actually done.

Mix the papers and read them aloud.

Everyone guesses who wrote what.

6. THE UNANSWERED QUESTION

Each person anonymously submits one question they've always wanted someone in the room to answer.

Shuffle the questions.

Players may answer or pass.

7. THE UNIVERSITY SCANDAL

Everyone anonymously submits their most ridiculous real university experience.

Read them aloud and vote for the most outrageous.

8. THE FINAL VERDICT

Everyone secretly votes on three categories:

- Most likely to accidentally start a university scandal.
- Most likely to fall in love with someone in this room.
- Most likely to have a secret relationship nobody knows about.

Reveal the winners.
